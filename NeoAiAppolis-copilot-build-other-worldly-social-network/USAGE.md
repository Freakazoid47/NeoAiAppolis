## Troubleshooting

**Colors not displaying?**
- Ensure your terminal supports ANSI colors
- Try a different terminal emulator

**Unicode characters broken?**
- Set your terminal encoding to UTF-8
- Use a font with good Unicode support

**Program too slow?**
- The network generates complex visualizations
- Reduce the number of entities in demos
- Skip continuous observation mode
